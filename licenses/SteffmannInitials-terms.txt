Kanzlei Initialen and Deutsche Zierschrift: the terms found, quoted as they stand
================================================================================

Both fonts were digitised by Dieter Steffmann (Typographer Mediengestaltung, Kreuztal, Germany). Scriptorium includes
them as gwern.net publishes them (one small TrueType font for each letter, A to Z), unchanged:
  https://gwern.net/static/font/dropcap/kanzlei/Kanzlei-Initialen-A.ttf  (… -Z.ttf)
  https://gwern.net/static/font/dropcap/de-zs/DeutscheZierschrift-A.ttf  (… -Z.ttf)
  https://github.com/gwern/gwern.net/tree/master/font/dropcap

No licence text is distributed with either font. These are the statements found on 2026-11-12, verbatim.

1. In the font files themselves (the OpenType "name" table)

   Kanzlei Initialen
     Copyright (name ID 0): "Copyright (c) Typographrer Mediengestaltung, 2002. All rights reserved. Digitized by
                             Dieter Steffmann, Kreuztal."
     License (name ID 13):  "have fun and enjoy"
     Embedding (OS/2 fsType): 0 (installable embedding: no restriction)

   Deutsche Zierschrift
     Copyright (name ID 0): "Copyright (c) Typographer Mediengestaltung, 2002. All rights reserved. Deutsche
                             Zierschrift geschaffen von Prof. Rudolf Koch, 1919-1921. Digitized and hinted by Dieter
                             Steffmann, Kreuztal."
     License (name ID 13):  "Have fun and enjoy"
     License URL (name ID 14): "http://www.steffmann.de"
     Embedding (OS/2 fsType): 0 (installable embedding: no restriction)

2. Dieter Steffmann, answering readers on his own site, http://www.steffmann.de/wordpress/ (the comments under
   "Startseite"). His answers have changed over the years; all of those found are given.

   16 December 2012:  "The fonts are free for private use only."
   19 May 2013:       "generally, all of the fonts are free for non commercial uses only to protect commercial font
                       shops (Linotype, Fontshop etc.). But many of the fonts are not present by the commercials, in
                       these cases i give the permission after single request."
   28 May 2014:       "download fonts at http://steffmann.1001fonts.com/ please. all of the fonts there can be
                       embedded!"
   12 February 2015:  "You may use all fonts for free, including commercial use."
   10 July 2015:      "Hi, You may use all fonts for free."
   28 August 2024:    "You may use all fonts for free."

3. Where the fonts are offered for download

   Deutsche Zierschrift, at https://www.dafont.com/deutsche-zierschrif.font (by Dieter Steffmann): "100% Free".
   Neither font was found in Dieter Steffmann's public listing at https://www.1001fonts.com/users/steffmann/ on that
   day. His fonts that are listed there (Goudy Initialen and Kanzlei among them) are offered under the "1001Fonts Free
   For Commercial Use License (FFC)", https://www.1001fonts.com/licenses/ffc.html, whose section 5 reads: "While the
   given typeface may freely be copied and passed along to other individuals for private use as its original
   downloadable zip-file, it may not be sold or published without written permission by its respective author."

None of these statements uses the word "redistribution". The two fonts are bundled at the repository owner's direction
(2026-11-12), on his reading that their terms permit it, as gwern.net publishes them. Anyone who passes Scriptorium on
should read the statements above for themselves. To leave the fonts out, delete
vault/.obsidian/plugins/scriptorium/dropcaps/kanzlei and .../de-zs: nothing else depends on them.
